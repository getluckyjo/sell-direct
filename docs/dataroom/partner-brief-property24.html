Why this is incremental for Property24

New inventory Your own private-listing tiers prove prime sellers want to sell without a mandate — but a self-managed ad is where many of those sales stall. We run the whole journey for them as registered practitioners and bring the homes to Property24 as one practitioner account with a clean, structured, agency-grade feed.
No cannibalisation Complementary, twice over. Your retail private-listing tiers (R499–R4,999) serve DIY sellers; we serve the run-it-for-me segment as a volume customer — both grow the private-sale audience on Property24. Full-service agencies’ stock — and their subscriptions — are untouched. This is audience Property24 adds, not audience it moves.
Better listings Every listing is validated end-to-end (erf, rates, levies, compliance status) with professional photography and floor plans — high-engagement stock in the price bands your advertisers want.
Demand loops back Our buyers start on Property24 — every listing carries a one-tap WhatsApp enquiry, and every completed sale is proof the portal works. Your traffic, our transaction rails.

The partnership we propose

1 · Tiered rates A single-account wholesale agreement priced per live listing, stepping down with committed volume. Your retail private listings are R2,299–R4,999 each; we bring a committed monthly pipeline through one practitioner account — wholesale economics for guaranteed inventory, squarely in the spirit of the Competition Commission’s OIPMI principle that portal pricing should be volume-fair, not size-punitive.
2 · Launch rate Your published 2026 card prices our launch profile (51–150 leads, R6m–R8m median band) at R7,153/month — the top price columns, for exactly the inventory the portal can’t source elsewhere. We’re asking to enter at SIBP level (R656/month) for the pilot and step up the published tiers at agreed lead milestones, so pricing tracks delivered volume, not projections.
3 · Success option We’re open to a success-based component — Property24’s own SBF model (R199 per sale registered at the Deeds Office) is a precedent we like: aligned incentives, zero risk to you.
4 · Clean feed Standard interoperability (per the OIPMI remedies, at no feed fee), one technical integration, one account manager — none of the admin overhead of hundreds of small accounts.

Why now

The private-sale segment is growing — your own private-listing tiers show the demand. What it lacks is practitioner-run, transaction-ready inventory; whoever hosts that first gets the listings, the audience and the story. We’re signing our portal partnerships for launch this quarter — we’d like Property24 to be first on the list.
