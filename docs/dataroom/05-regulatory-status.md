# Sold Direct — Regulatory Status & Portal Access (as at 26 Aug 2026)

Written to be read by an investor before they ask. Two things gate trading: a **Fidelity Fund
Certificate** and a **portal subscription**. Both are in progress; neither is in place.

## 1. Property practitioner registration — the critical path

**Status: not held. Registration in progress.**

Sold Direct is not yet a registered property practitioner and does not hold a Fidelity Fund
Certificate (FFC). Until it does, the company cannot lawfully take a mandate, and — under the
Property Practitioners Act — **a practitioner without a valid FFC is not entitled to
remuneration**, a provision South African courts have enforced by clawing back commission
already earned.

**The 0% model does not avoid this.** Three independent limbs bring us inside the Act:

1. The test is *acquisition of gain*, not who pays. We earn origination commission on the
   transaction.
2. Marketing and publicly exhibiting property for sale on a seller's mandate is itself the
   regulated activity — and Property24's subscription requires a mandate for every listing.
3. The PPA's definition expressly extends to procuring or facilitating **financing** in
   connection with a property sale, which captures bond origination — our core revenue line.

The **1% Flex fee** is, additionally, remuneration paid by a seller for practitioner services.

⚠ **A written attorney opinion on the above is outstanding** and should be obtained before the
round closes. This document is the founders' working understanding, not legal advice.

### Route to the certificate

A firm FFC requires a qualified **principal property practitioner** (NQF5 + PDE5). Qualifying a
founder from scratch runs 12+ months through the candidate period; appointing an already-
qualified principal compresses that to weeks. The latter is the route being pursued —
a supervisory engagement is under discussion with a candidate holding ~20 years of prime
Cape Town experience (Sotheby's, Pam Golding), targeted to start on close of the round.

Also required: SARS tax clearance, a BEE certificate (EME affidavit under R10m turnover), and a
trust-account arrangement. Because deposits and purchase funds are held in the **conveyancing
attorney's** trust account and never by Sold Direct, a **trust-account exemption** is expected to
apply — to be confirmed in the application.

**Mitigation of key-person risk:** the certificate would initially rest on one external
appointee. Both founders studied property-related degrees at UCT and should begin their own
practitioner qualifications in parallel so the company is not indefinitely dependent on a single
individual's registration.

### What the public sites say

Until registration completes, the marketing site states plainly that registration is in progress
and that **no mandate will be accepted until it and a valid FFC are in place**. The AI concierge
is instructed never to claim the company is already registered. A prior claim to hold an FFC was
removed on 26 Aug 2026.

## 2. Property24 — agency subscription

**Status: paperwork received, not yet signed** (gated on the FFC — Annexure D requires the
number). Full 2026 rate card, subscription agreement and annexures are on file.

**The model is contractually permitted.** Clause 2(a) entitles a subscriber to list
**unlimited** properties for sale, *"provided the Subscriber possesses a mandate to market each
property which is listed"* — precisely our structure. No cap applies to us as the agency, and no
special arrangement is needed to execute the portal strategy.

**Pricing is by lead volume × median price bracket**, invoiced monthly in advance:

| Monthly leads (R6m–R8m bracket) | Fee (ex VAT) |
|---|---:|
| 1–10 | R989 |
| 11–30 | R2,868 |
| 51–150 *(≈17 concurrent listings — Y1 profile)* | **R7,153** |
| 251–500 | R13,251 |
| SIBP (small independent business package) | **R656** |

This reconciles with the model's portal COGS: ~R1,700 per registered deal in Y1 falling to
~R300 by Y5. All prices exclude VAT.

**Open commercial points, raised with Property24 and unanswered at the date of this document:**

1. **Clause 10** prices a subscription per *"front door" or area agent*. Sold Direct operates one
   entity from a single Cape Town office covering multiple suburbs. Written confirmation of a
   single subscription is required — a per-area reading would multiply portal COGS.
2. **Annexure P ("Package")** overrides both pricing and the one-month termination under clauses
   3 and 9. It was not included in the pack; terms requested.
3. **Opening median price bracket** for a subscriber with no prior-year lead history.
4. **Feed-in via API** as our own technology partner, in place of PropCtrl at R672/month.

**Position:** sign the standard agreement to unblock launch — at pilot volumes the fee is
immaterial — and open the package negotiation once real lead volume gives us something to
negotiate with. The relationship is framed as complementary: Property24's retail private-listing
tiers (R499–R4,999) serve DIY sellers, while Sold Direct is a practitioner-run service and a
paying wholesale customer of their distribution.
