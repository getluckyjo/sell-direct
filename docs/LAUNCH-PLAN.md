# Sold Direct — Cape Town Launch Plan

> **The core of this plan is one structural gap:** South Africa's property portals are
> effectively closed to prime private sellers. Property24's private self-service channel is
> capped at **R500,000** (2026 rate card / private-listing terms) — above that, only a
> registered practitioner account gets a home onto the portal where ~all buyers search.
> The cap is structural, not accidental: it protects P24's agency-subscription revenue, so
> it is very unlikely to be lifted. **Sold Direct — a PPRA-registered practitioner with a
> 0% model — is the compliant bridge across that gap.** Everything below is built on it.

## 1. The wedge, stated plainly

| | |
|---|---|
| **The gap** | A R6m Camps Bay owner who wants to sell privately cannot get onto Property24. Their choice today: mandate an agent (~R414k commission) or be invisible to buyers. |
| **Why we fill it** | Practitioner status (PPRA, FFC) gives us full portal access; the 0% model means the seller gives up nothing to get it; WhatsApp does the admin. |
| **Why it's defensible** | P24 can't lift the cap without a war with its paying agencies. A copycat needs practitioner registration, a portal agreement, and consumer trust — none of which is fast. |
| **The message** | *"Your home on Property24 — without giving up your private sale."* |

## 2. Message hierarchy (every channel, this order)

1. **Portal access** — "Your home on the portals, normally closed to private sellers."
2. **0% commission** — "The banks pay us, not you." (1% disclosed for cash/Flex.)
3. **Guided & compliant** — WhatsApp concierge + registered practitioners, mandate to registration.

The 0% story is what we *are*; the portal story is why a prime seller acts **today**.

## 3. Sequence

**P0 · Unlock the wedge (weeks 1–4) — nothing launches until this is signed**
- Property24 syndication agreement — enter at SIBP (R656/m), step up published tiers at lead milestones (brief: `dataroom/partner-brief-property24.html`).
- Private Property as second portal (same OIPMI interoperability rights).
- Confirm practitioner registration + FFC in the operating entity; BetterBond referral letter; panel-conveyancer subscription.

**P1 · Founding twenty (weeks 4–10)**
- 20 founding listings across four prime pockets: Atlantic Seaboard, City Bowl, Southern Suburbs, Constantia.
- White-glove concierge; promise: *live on Property24 within 48 hours of mandate*.
- Every founding listing is a case study in the making (before/after: "invisible → on the portal at 0%").

**P2 · Prove the loop (weeks 10–20)**
- First registered sales; measure: listing→enquiry→OTP→registration conversion, leads/listing (this drives our P24 tier cost), sell-through, CAC by channel, % of sellers citing portal access as the trigger.
- Publish the first "sold direct" stories (with consent).

**P3 · Scale (month 5+)**
- ATL brand spend unlocks **only after** P2 conversion data validates the model's ATL→deals assumption (model watch-out #2).
- Broaden suburbs; PR the wedge story nationally.

## 4. Channels, ranked by fit with the wedge

1. **The portal itself** — every live listing advertises us to the exact audience (prime buyers *and* watching neighbours-who-are-next-sellers).
2. **Suburb communities** — WhatsApp/community groups and referral loops in the four launch pockets; prime sells on word of mouth.
3. **PR** — "the R500k rule nobody knows about" is a genuinely newsworthy consumer story; we are its answer.
4. **Targeted social** to homeowners in launch suburbs (interest + geo).
5. **ATL** — gated behind P2 (see above).

## 5. Targets & kill criteria

| Metric | Target | Kill/act trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Founding listings | 20 by week 10 | <10 → revisit offer/segment before spending further |
| Attribution | ≥40% of sellers cite portal access | <20% → wedge is weaker than modelled; re-test messaging |
| Leads per listing | ≥5/month (also sets the P24 tier) | Tier cost >R10k/m before 100 concurrent listings → renegotiate |
| Sell-through | ≥60% within 6 months | Below → pricing-guidance & concierge intervention |
| Effective CAC (organic phase) | <R3,000/listing | Above → channels 1–3 not working; do not proceed to ATL |

## 6. Wedge-specific risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| P24 declines or slow-walks the syndication agreement | Sign Private Property first (also bound by OIPMI interoperability); own listing pages + shareable WhatsApp links carry the interim; the CompCom remedies are the escalation backdrop, never the opening threat. |
| P24 raises the private cap | Structurally unlikely (agency conflict). If it happens, our concierge + practitioners + 0% still beat a raw self-service ad — the wedge narrows, the moat of service remains. |
| Copycat practitioner-FSBO hybrids | Move fast, own the prime brand, and keep the WhatsApp product depth (state machine, bond rails) they'd have to rebuild. |
