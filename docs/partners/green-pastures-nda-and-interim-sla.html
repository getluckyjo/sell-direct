1. Purpose and Structure of this Agreement

1.1  The Parties are in discussions regarding a proposed exclusive relationship under which Green Pastures would provide mortuary services and cremation services to or for the benefit of the Company (the “Proposed Relationship”).

1.2  The Parties wish to (a) record their intent to negotiate the Proposed Relationship in good faith, (b) protect confidential information exchanged during those discussions and any interim services, and (c) agree a basic interim service framework that applies until a full, definitive service level agreement is concluded (the “Definitive Agreement”).

1.3  Binding and non-binding provisions. The following clauses are legally binding on the Parties: clause 3 (Confidentiality), clause 4 (Interim Service Levels, to the extent services are actually rendered), clause 5 (Exclusive Negotiation), clause 6 (Data Protection), clause 7 (Term and Termination) and clause 8 (General). Clause 2 (Statement of Intent) and all commercial terms of the Proposed Relationship (including pricing, volumes and final service levels) are non-binding statements of current intent only and remain subject to negotiation and to the conclusion of the Definitive Agreement.

2. Statement of Intent (non-binding)

2.1  The Parties record their present intention that, subject to agreement on terms:

(a)  Green Pastures would be appointed as the Company’s exclusive provider of mortuary services (including collection, transport, storage and preparation of the deceased) and cremation services in (agreed service area, e.g. the Western Cape);

(b)  the appointment would be for an initial term of months/years, with pricing, volumes, service standards and review mechanisms to be set out in the Definitive Agreement; and

(c)  the Parties will use reasonable endeavours to conclude the Definitive Agreement within 30 (thirty) days of the Signature Date, or such longer period as they may agree in writing.

2.2  Nothing in this clause 2 obliges either Party to conclude the Definitive Agreement, and no binding exclusive appointment arises until the Definitive Agreement is signed.

3. Confidentiality (binding — mutual NDA)

3.1  “Confidential Information” means all information disclosed by either Party (the “Discloser”) to the other (the “Recipient”) in connection with the Proposed Relationship, whether before or after the Signature Date and in whatever form, including: business plans, strategies, pricing, costs, financial information, customer and supplier details, operational methods, facilities information, know-how, and the existence and content of the Parties’ discussions and of this Agreement.

3.2  Confidential Information excludes information which: (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement; (b) was lawfully known to the Recipient before disclosure; (c) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction; (d) is independently developed without use of the Discloser’s Confidential Information; or (e) must be disclosed by law, regulation or court order (in which case the Recipient shall, where lawful, give the Discloser prompt written notice before disclosing).

3.3  The Recipient shall: (a) use Confidential Information solely to evaluate, negotiate and perform the Proposed Relationship; (b) keep it confidential and protect it with at least the care it applies to its own confidential information, and no less than reasonable care; (c) disclose it only to its directors, employees, and professional advisers who need to know it for the purpose in clause 3.3(a) and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less strict than this clause 3; and (d) not copy it except as reasonably required for that purpose.

3.4  On the Discloser’s written request, or on termination of discussions, the Recipient shall return or destroy all Confidential Information (and certify destruction if asked), save for copies required by law or automatic electronic archiving, which remain subject to this clause 3.

3.5  The obligations in this clause 3 commence on the Signature Date and survive for 3 (three) years after termination of this Agreement, and indefinitely in respect of personal information governed by clause 6.

3.6  Each Party acknowledges that damages may be an inadequate remedy for breach of this clause 3 and that the Discloser may seek interdictory or other urgent relief in addition to any other remedy.

5. Exclusive Negotiation (binding)

5.1  For 30 (thirty) days from the Signature Date (the “Exclusivity Period”), and unless extended by written agreement:

(a)  the Company shall not solicit or enter into negotiations with any other provider for the exclusive supply of mortuary and cremation services in the intended service area; and

(b)  Green Pastures shall not solicit or enter into negotiations with any competitor of the Company for an arrangement that would prevent or materially impair the Proposed Relationship.

5.2  Either Party may terminate the Exclusivity Period on 14 (fourteen) days’ written notice if, in its reasonable opinion, the negotiations are no longer likely to result in the Definitive Agreement.

6. Data Protection (binding)

6.1  Each Party shall comply with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 4 of 2013 (POPIA) in respect of any personal information (including that of deceased persons’ next of kin and special personal information) processed in connection with this Agreement.

6.2  Each Party shall: (a) process personal information only as necessary for the purposes of this Agreement; (b) implement appropriate, reasonable technical and organisational security measures; (c) notify the other Party without undue delay of any security compromise affecting shared personal information; and (d) not retain personal information longer than lawfully required.

7. Term and Termination

7.1  This Agreement commences on the Signature Date and continues until the earlier of: (a) the signature of the Definitive Agreement (which will supersede it); or (b) termination by either Party on 30 (thirty) days’ written notice; or (c) termination by written agreement.

7.2  Termination does not affect: accrued rights; fees due for Interim Services already rendered; or clauses 3 (Confidentiality), 6 (Data Protection) and 8 (General), which survive termination.

8. General

8.1  No partnership or agency. Nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture, employment or agency relationship between the Parties.

8.2  Costs. Each Party bears its own costs of negotiating this Agreement and the Definitive Agreement.

8.3  Whole agreement; variation. This Agreement is the whole agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and may only be varied in writing signed by both Parties.

8.4  No cession. Neither Party may cede, assign or delegate its rights or obligations without the other’s prior written consent.
